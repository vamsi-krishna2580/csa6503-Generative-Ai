Enter the query requirement: Find students who scored above 80 in DBMS.

Generated SQL:

```sql
SELECT S.Name
FROM Student AS S
JOIN Marks AS M
  ON S.StudentID = M.StudentID
JOIN Course AS C
  ON M.CourseID = C.CourseID
WHERE
  C.CourseName = 'DBMS' AND M.Marks > 80;
```
